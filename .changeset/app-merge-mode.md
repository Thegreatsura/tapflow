Settings → Apps has a merge mode: drag an app's bundle ID row onto another app to move that platform's ID and builds there, from the mouse or the keyboard. Renaming and deleting an app moved into each app's ⋯ menu there. App Center lists both bundle IDs of an app whose iOS and Android IDs differ, and opens the first app when the URL names one a merge removed.
