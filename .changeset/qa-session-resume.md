A tester can come back to a QA session. The QA page's URL carries `session` and `device` once a device starts; opening or reloading it rejoins that device, keeping the installed app, when the device is waiting for that tester. A device that is off, or on and not theirs, is offered for a fresh start instead of being booted unasked. The relay remembers who a device is waiting for after a person's connection drops, for as long as the existing idle countdown runs, and the device listing says so: `waiting` (the person's `name` — display name, else the email's local part — and email as `user`, with `remainingMs`, and `aiHolding` while an AI client borrows the device) to everyone, and only to that person `resumable`, `releasedAgoMs`, `waiting.yours` and `waiting.buildId`; `heldBySelf` when another tab of the asker's account holds the device live; and `endedBy` (`name` and email) when someone else took or shut down the device while it waited, kept on every listing until the idle window passes. A shutdown counts only once it is forwarded to the agent, and the countdown is cancelled when the device reports it is down rather than when the shutdown is sent. The dashboard shows a notice for an interrupted session on App Center and the QA page, asks before a teammate takes a device someone is coming back to, and tells the owner who took it when they come back within the idle window. A device waiting for a person is not a candidate for the relay's capacity shutdowns, as a person's countdown is already not one for a lease, and a `device:shutdown-done` that arrives after a boot went out behind its shutdown no longer ends the wait and countdown that boot's session started.
